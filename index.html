Kazi Noshin

I am a PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), USA. During my MS at the University of Virginia, I worked with Prof. Aidong Zhang. Before starting my graduate studies, I worked as a lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at University of Asia Pacific, Bangladesh, and as a research assistant in the BUET-Japan Institute of Disaster Prevention and Urban Safety (BUET-JIDPUS), where I worked under the supervision of Prof. Mohammed Eunus Ali.

I received a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology. In my final year, I did my thesis in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) under the guidance of Associate Prof. Dr. Mohammad Saifur Rahman.

My primary research interest is human-computer interaction (HCI), especially areas that address human well-being and behavior, including ICTD, CSCW, human-AI interaction, social computing, human-centered ML design, and ethics.
